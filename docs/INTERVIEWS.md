# Five short applicant interview questions

Prepared for later conversations with adult applicants. No one has been contacted; there are no participant answers or findings. Ask about actual experiences, allow skipped questions, and avoid collecting application documents or identifying details.

1. Tell me about your most recent scholarship application. What was hardest?
2. What work, if any, did you miss or have to repeat?
3. How often have instructions changed after you started, and how did you notice?
4. What tools did you use to track instructions, tasks, and completed work?
5. What took effort when keeping your plan current or returning after a break?

Capture concrete examples, including “this never happened” and cases where current tools already work well. Do not turn opinions about the preview into evidence of changed instructions, product benefit, or willingness to pay. Human evaluation belongs to later sessions and requires real observations.
